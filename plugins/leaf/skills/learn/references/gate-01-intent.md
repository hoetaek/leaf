# ① Intent

**What ① locks: the why — the problem definition — separated from the surface
request, with the what still provisional.** The why is the question furthest
from any solution, so it carries the least solution-contamination; stating it
derives or corrects the what, and the gap between derived intent and surface
request is ①'s first discovery.

Preserve what the user actually said, then follow the why to where it lands: a
problem definition, an external obligation with the deeper why asked, curiosity
locked as exploratory, a felt sense deferred to ② — or the work is killed here.
Competing whys derive competing whats; a why that embeds a diagnosis keeps the
hypothesis out of the what until ② verifies it.

Gate to continue:

- Raw wording is captured, and the locked intent is stated separately from it —
  not a restatement of the user's implementation-shaped request.
- The why has been followed to where it actually lands, and is sharp enough
  that an artifact answering a different question could be recognized as wrong.
- The provisional what names the intended artifact or result clearly enough to
  learn what it needs — and stays provisional: the work may still change shape
  or die.

Helpful defaults — why/what field layout, split signals: the `learn` skill.
Defaults, never pass/fail conditions.

Adding a check to a gate requires recording, next to it, the actual failure it
prevents. Promoting a default into a pass/fail condition counts as adding a
check.

The why locked here is itself a hypothesis. Return when ② learning overturns
the stated need, ④'s concrete instance shows the problem was misstated, or
⑧ drafting / ⑨ review reveals the work answers a different question — re-lock
the intent explicitly, then resume only the gates that depended on what
changed.
